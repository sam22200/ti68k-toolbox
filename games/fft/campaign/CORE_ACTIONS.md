# Shared primary effects and impact ownership

First L2 slice,2026-10-25. Jump refinements remain deferred by user priority.
This checkpoint is a refactor of currently supported actions, not completion of
the status system or a source certificate for multistrike/reaction order.

## Runtime change

`action_effect.h` owns pure target HP saturation and existing status changes.
Commit and AI use those same functions; healing forecasts use the same HP cap.
The existing formula evaluator still supplies hit chance and amount. Prediction
does not mutate HP/statuses or consume items; RNG remains in the action caller.

`action_resolve.inc` separates impact commit from `attack_step` presentation.
Commit runs once at ACT_HIT after a successful roll, retaining current order:
primary effect and recoil, current drain/cross extras, rewards, Wish self-cost,
then turn-list refresh when the previous primary/user rule requires it. Missed
items remain consumed at action start. Defend retains its command sentinel;
stat raise/break retain their existing cap/floor. Terminal checks remain at
the settled end of the animation/reward interval, not inside an HP assignment.

On TI the FORMULA image owns impact commit and calls resident rewards/placement
callbacks. Its extra-effect formula calls stay local: it never evicts itself.
AI keeps its own compiled copy of the pure rules/formulas and never swaps out
an executing AI image to predict a candidate. PC and legacy builds compile the
identical source directly. ABI8 adds one rewards callback; all9 FMB2 images are
rebuilt and validated. No new State, Work, Save, queue or heap field is added.

## Evidence and scope

`check_actions.py` checks recorded primary effects from the existing13 original
skill cases (`oracle.h`, generated by `tools/oracle.py --skills`), feeding each
observed execution amount rather than pretending to reproduce the PS1 RNG.
It covers item use on misses, ailments removed/preserved, healing saturation,
revival/death-counter reset, Wish, Defend, raise/break bounds, one-time damage/
rewards, delayed terminal checks, drain/cross-extra relocation and three complete
AI rankings. The295 assertions plus forecast/ranking hashes pass on PC,
sanitizers and actual68000, agreeing with a captured pre-change build.

`benchmark_actions.py` stages that explicit local source/bank snapshot in a
temporary tree and compares old/current rules plus six complete framebuffer
hashes and all enabled ability forecasts. Attack, Potion, cure, revival, Wish and Defend
pictures/text are identical before/after and PC/TI. No gallery images need to be
republished or approved. Existing Charge cases, module validation and recoil
callers are checked separately; the full census/decoder/input sweep is not run.

The23 timed impacts cost163242→211530 datasheet cycles in total (mean7097→9196,
peak20832→33480; including the runner's existing AMS-call estimates). The
relocation adds a bounded image copy on a cold hit and
some shared-dispatch cost; it does not make impact processing faster. These
are isolated commit timings, not a worst-case whole-frame or FPS certificate.
