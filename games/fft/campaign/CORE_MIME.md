# Automatic Mime imitation

The generic Mime job93 now imitates accepted allied primary actions after their
strikes, reactions and EXP/JP settle. It introduces no player command or new
renderer. The20 generic jobs retain201/201 actions and78/78 R/S/M consumers;
Mime is an integration feature outside the four50-ID delivery blocks.

## Execution

The accepted root records its original facing, source camp (player and guest share it), action/set, weapon
pair and target offset before the native action pose turns the actor. Actual
deferred effects record on resolution, never when arming. The queue freezes
present Mime slots, then rechecks live job/team/status/terrain in slot order.
Mimes do not record another donor. Reactions remain bounded secondary packets;
each imitation is its own primary root, so ordinary reactions and rewards work.
The original turn actor, movement/action flags and due-action continuation stay
owned by the original turn. Victory/defeat is checked after the queue drains.

Target offsets rotate in world space through the measured source/native facing
permutation (3,1,2,0), independently of the camera. Terrain layers use the
original integer-height proximity/higher-tie choice; Jump retains its existing
same-layer adaptation. There is no fallback to the donor's victim when the
translated scalar panel is empty. Such an imitation is skipped without a new
failure animation. Ordinary ranged attacks recheck their trajectory after the
cold selector returns; one rejected shot is processed per update.

A Mime temporarily borrows the accepted weapon pair and action menu, using its
own stats, innate traits, HP/MP and statuses. Its ordinary attack/Charge hand
count comes from its own Two Swords capability. Equipment/menu are restored
only after all responses/rewards of that imitation settle. No learned slots,
MP, second consumable, thrown weapon or katana stock are required. Restrictions
such as Frog/Silence and source CANNOT_MIMIC still apply; that flag is byte5,
not byte4 (AoE). Item/Throw/Jump/Charge menus follow the source exception.

Math keeps its original criterion without borrowing learning. Elements chooses
the Mime's own surface ability. Draw Out and global performances centre on the
Mime; a copied performance is one immediate pulse. Jump/Charge execute their
prepared effect immediately and never arm a Mime timer. The source oracle
isolates preview effect preparation, so this does not certify all original
performance/Jump animation states.
