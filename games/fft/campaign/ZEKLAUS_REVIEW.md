### Additional reference: adamrt/fft-software-renderer

Inspected on2026-11-23 at upstream commit
`537d5718c4481b198ef26019df1e98d82250d5ac`. This is an optional independent
PC reference, not a new build dependency or installed tool. Its
[reader](https://github.com/adamrt/fft-software-renderer/blob/537d5718c4481b198ef26019df1e98d82250d5ac/fft_reader.go)
parses GNS, mesh normals, textures/palettes and source light/background records.
Its [camera](https://github.com/adamrt/fft-software-renderer/blob/537d5718c4481b198ef26019df1e98d82250d5ac/camera.go)
supports orthographic and perspective inspection. These can help cross-check
future source assets, framing and lighting before four-grey adaptation.

It is not an exact original-game oracle: the
[engine](https://github.com/adamrt/fft-software-renderer/blob/537d5718c4481b198ef26019df1e98d82250d5ac/engine.go)
documents average-depth painter sorting artifacts, and the
[history](https://github.com/adamrt/fft-software-renderer/blob/537d5718c4481b198ef26019df1e98d82250d5ac/HISTORY.md)
states uncertainty about lighting accuracy and uses float64 throughout. Our
inference: useful as a second offline reference; it supplies no direct native
RAM/FPS gain and does not replace measured68000 rendering. No upstream code
was copied, installed or run to produce the new color reference.
